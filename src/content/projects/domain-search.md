Search and listings sit at the centre of a property marketplace. I led the engineering team responsible for these experiences across web and mobile web.

## Success

Domain.com.au residential listings see over 40 million monthly visits. We were in charge of the highest traffic pages in the company including residential search and listings pages. The team kept shipping valuable and impactful product improvements while meeting the operational demands of a high-traffic consumer service.

## My contribution

Contributed collaboratively in a PM, EM, design triad. I led the engineering team across desktop and mobile web, balancing product delivery with individual contributions. Fast delivery, analyse data and repeat was a muscle that became very strong.

## What we built

My team added a new photo gallery, map search and school catchments to Domain.com.au web. [Here's an example listing](https://www.domain.com.au/4-mackillop-place-mount-eliza-vic-3930-2021062575).
