I led a games team of five engineers and five designers working across [Reading Eggs](https://readingeggs.com.au/), Mathseeds, and Reading Eggspress.

## Success

Reading Eggs has been used by more than 20 million children globally. It’s available in 75 countries and is a great Australian success story. The work of my team ensured a bright future for Reading Eggs to work on the iPad, on the web and everywhere, as the original Flash software quickly became obsolete.

## My contribution

I led the games team of 5 engineers and 5 designers through the rebuild. Finding suitable HTML5 technologies to closely replicate what we had previously built in Flash. Including audio, video, spritesheet animations, WebGL and memory management. Game libraries like Phaser didn’t exist back then, so we built our own called Caper. The work demanded shared components, careful performance, and an experience that felt good on both desktop and mobile devices.

## What we built

The centrepiece was rebuilding Reading Eggs from Flash to HTML5: 120 lessons and the complete games arcade.
