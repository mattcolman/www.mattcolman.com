Building an AI product asks a team to learn while it ships. This talk covers the practical work of creating that team when nobody begins as the expert.

It draws on real experience leading engineers into AI product development: learning how to evaluate uncertain systems, sharing knowledge quickly, and keeping the work grounded in value for users.
